
### Kit 
- Passive 
	- deal bonus magic damage 
	- if he is below 50%, he heals the same amount 
	- if he is below 25%, he heals 3x the amount
- Q : Lunges and bites, deals damage based on targets max hp and heals damage dealt
- W  
	- passive, senses champs below 50% hp. Gains movement speed when moving towards them and bonus attack speed 
	- active, gain these bonuses regardless of health to the closest champ 
- E : damage reduction for 2.5 seconds, when reactivated he fears enemies in a circle near him
- R : Straight line skill shot, heals a portion of health and range is decided from movement speed
Skill order : W > Q > E 

**CLEAR SPEED = ~2:50 CAN REACH CRAB ON TIME WITH 1 SMITE** 

### CD
Q -> 8 / 7.5 / 7 / 6.5 / 6 
W -> 80 / 70 / 60 / 50 / 40  (reduced to 30% if no targets are found)
E -> 15 / 14 / 13 / 12 / 11 
R -> 110 / 90 / 70


### Strengths
- Strong early game jungler
- can take objectives easily
- good ganker 
- can solo objectives easily 
- strong in 1v1 (better try not to side lane against him)

### Weaknesses 
- slow clear speed
- anti heal (hard counter)
- falls off late 
	- needs to hit ult on a good target to be useful 
- if you flash his r or have qss he is useless

### Strategy 
#### For ganks / early game
- try to avoid and play safe 
- try to invade when he is over committing on ganks 
- flash as soon as you see him in lane 
- place vision in river to avoid ganks
- **Avoid long fights and kite with slows. Short trades are good against him**
#### Mid game 
- Try to group as much as you can 
- build anti heal early (maybe just a component if he is fed)
- Respect the health threshold
- kite him and don't rush fights 
- **focus on kiting and precent health damage. Avoid fighting in choke points** 
#### Late game 
- Warwick is at his weakest 
- play to group and make sure to have anti heal 
- he will always try to take someone 1v1 and is useless in team fight 
- try not be picked off by him 
- **Warwick is very durable late game, if he is too tanky focus squishy then warwick last**
### Power spikes 
- lv 3 : full kit, can gank and when you can all in
- lv 6 : biggest power spike , can all in almost any champ in the game 
- lv 11/16 : can start ganking more and fight more 
- Biggest spike is in the first 2 items 
- ~20 mins he is strongest in the game
